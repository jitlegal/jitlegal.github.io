Lead Feed privacy policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Who we are

Lead Feed is operated by JIT Legal ("we"). Contact: jitlegalco@gmail.com.

What information we use

  • Public trademark records. We process records published by the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office, such as application numbers, marks, statuses, deadlines, and owner names and locations as they appear in those public records.
  • Subscriber information. For law firms that subscribe, we keep the firm name, the names and email addresses of its users, bar license details they provide, their plan settings, and the follow-up status they record on their leads.

How we use Google user data

Lead Feed's Google integration requests a single permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, for our own operating Google account. We use it only to:

  • create spreadsheets that list a subscribing firm's leads,
  • share those spreadsheets with that firm's users, and
  • read and update those same spreadsheets to sync the firm's follow-up status.

This permission only covers files the app itself created. The app cannot see, open, or change any other files in Google Drive.

Lead Feed's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not use it to train AI models.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. A firm's leads and spreadsheets are shared only with that firm's users. We use service providers to run the service (for example, database hosting, email delivery, and Google Workspace for spreadsheets), only as needed to provide it.

Retention and deletion

Lead records are removed when they are no longer stuck (for example, once an attorney is appointed or a deadline has passed). When a firm stops using Lead Feed, we stop sharing its spreadsheet and remove its account data on request. Anyone appearing in the public records may ask us to stop including their application by emailing us.

Security

Access is limited to authorized users, credentials are stored encrypted or as one-way hashes, and connections use encryption in transit.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change, and change the effective date above.
