Lead Feed privacy policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Who we are

Lead Feed is operated by JIT Legal ("we"). Contact: jitlegalco@gmail.com.

What information we use

  • Public trademark records. We process records published by the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office, such as application numbers, marks, statuses, deadlines, and owner names and locations as they appear in those public records.
  • Subscriber information. For law firms that subscribe, we keep the firm name, the names and email addresses of its users, bar license details they provide, their plan settings, and the follow-up status they record on their leads.

Google user data

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and email domain from Google and use them only to sign you in and connect you to your firm's account. If your firm receives its leads as a Google spreadsheet, Lead Feed creates that spreadsheet, shares it with your firm's users, and reads and updates it to keep your follow-up status in sync. It can access only spreadsheets it created, not any other files.

We use Google user data only to provide these features. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. We share it only with your firm's users and with service providers that help us run Lead Feed, and we delete it on request or when your firm stops using Lead Feed.

Lead Feed's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. A firm's leads are shared only with that firm's users. We use service providers to host and operate Lead Feed, only as needed to provide it.

Retention and deletion

Lead records are removed when they are no longer stuck (for example, once an attorney is appointed or a deadline has passed). When a firm stops using Lead Feed, we stop sharing its spreadsheet and remove its account data on request. Anyone appearing in the public records may ask us to stop including their application by emailing us.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including limiting access to authorized users and encrypting data in transit.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change, and change the effective date above.
