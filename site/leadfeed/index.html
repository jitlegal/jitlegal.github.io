Lead Feed

Lead Feed gives licensed trademark attorneys a daily list of U.S. trademark applications that were filed without an attorney and are now stuck: the USPTO has raised an issue with a response deadline, the application was recently abandoned and can still be revived, or it is suspended.

How it works

  • Data comes only from public records published by the United States Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO).
  • Subscribing law firms receive their leads by email and in a spreadsheet shared with them, where they track their own follow-up.
  • Lead Feed never contacts applicants. Law firms decide whether and how to reach out, under their own professional rules.
  • Lead Feed is not affiliated with the USPTO and does not provide legal advice.
